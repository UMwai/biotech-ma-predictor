# Historical control feasibility receipts

This directory contains **20 historical membership-event candidates, two observed positive acquisition announcements, one reviewed non-acquisition window, and 17 unknown outcome windows**. No complete training observation is available. The original `window_review_receipts.json` preserves the earlier 18-unknown feasibility checkpoint; the ARQT follow-up below supersedes that company's outcome review.

`arqt_archive_followup.json` enumerates all 91 entries exposed by Arcutis's 2020/2021 corporate archive and records substantive reviews of 45 full release bodies, including 43 within December 21, 2020–December 21, 2021 and two boundary neighbors. No qualifying target-side control announcement or censoring event was found in those bodies. All 55 downloaded source hashes are verified. Coverage means the designated issuer archive as retrieved, not a census of regulatory filings, removed releases or all third-party news.

`arqt_listing_review.json` records the issuer's explicit Nasdaq common-equity trading history and original pre-observation biotech description. Its SHA identifies a browser review receipt; original SEC HTML bytes were not downloaded. `review_inputs.json` binds both review files, and `scripts/build_history_seed.py` verifies their underlying evidence before generating `reviewed_controls.json` and updating local progress. Raw downloads remain local under `raw_arqt_archive_followup/` and are excluded from published desk snapshots.

This assembled label becomes available at its actual September 2026 review time. It cannot enter earlier historical training folds. Pre-observation pending-deal eligibility and contemporaneous features remain unassembled, so `risk_set_eligible` is unknown and the complete training-observation count stays zero.

`membership_event_candidates.csv` names twenty securities from Nasdaq's December 2020 NBI addition notice. `window_review_receipts.json` records proposed December 21, 2020–December 21, 2021 review windows, identity evidence where verified, bounded issuer reviews, and reasons the records remain ineligible. `source_catalog.json` distinguishes browser-reviewed evidence from actual downloaded payloads. `download_receipts.json` records both successful downloads and concrete access failures. All changes are confined to this directory.

## What the historical sources establish

Nasdaq's [December 11, 2020 reconstitution notice](https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/annual-changes-nasdaq-biotechnology-index-6) identifies named additions and removals scheduled before the December 21 session. It is genuine dated exchange evidence and includes companies absent from today's surviving-company lists. The selected names include Akouos, Applied Molecular Transport, Calithera, Clovis, Concert, Constellation and Five Prime. The notice records an announced index change; actual execution, complete historical baskets, common-equity status and uninterrupted listing intervals need additional evidence.

Nasdaq's [2021 reconstitution research](https://indexes.nasdaq.com/docs/NBI%20Research%20-%20December%202021%20Reconstitution.pdf), pages 1–2, reports 36 total deletions in 2020. The December 2020 annual notice lists only 16 removals. Thus annual notices alone omit intra-year changes and cannot reconstruct complete membership by carrying names forward. A raw copy of this official research PDF is saved and hashed.

The [official NBI weighting page](https://indexes.nasdaq.com/Index/Weighting/NBI) exposes a date field but requires login for full access; the unauthenticated response did not contain a historical constituent file. Nasdaq [documents historical components and corporate actions through GIW](https://www.nasdaq.com/products/global-indexes/data/giw). No account was created or subscription purchased. Free individual historical notices are available; an authenticated complete constituent history was not obtained.

## Material company examples

- **Arcutis / ARQT / CIK 1787306:** The [2021 issuer 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1787306/000178730622000009/arqt-20211231.htm) explicitly asserts Nasdaq common-stock trading since January 31, 2020 and presents performance through December 31, 2021. The conservative interior listing interval covers the proposed horizon. A separate corporate archive supplied the completed follow-up after the original investor archive failed; its scoped negative outcome is saved as described above.
- **Crinetics / CRNX / CIK 1658247:** The [2021 issuer 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1658247/000095017022005050/crnx-20211231.htm) confirms Nasdaq common stock. Its Radionetics disclosures concern licensing technology to a new company and receiving equity, not selling Crinetics. Browser access to the archive omitted the historical inventory; direct access returned HTTP 403. Search-index headlines cannot establish completeness, so the window remains unknown.
- **Beam / BEAM / CIK 1745999:** The [February 23, 2021 release](https://investors.beamtx.com/news-releases/news-release-details/beam-therapeutics-announces-acquisition-guide-therapeutics) identifies Beam as purchaser of Guide Therapeutics. It must not become an acquisition-of-Beam positive. It also does not prove that no other target-side announcement occurred during the year. The full archive was not enumerated.
- **Five Prime / FPRX** and **Constellation / CNST:** Their [March 4, 2021 Amgen announcement](https://www.amgen.com/newsroom/press-releases/2021/03/amgen-to-acquire-five-prime-therapeutics-for-%241-9-billion-in-cash) and [June 2, 2021 MorphoSys announcement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1434418/000119312521180060/d182948dex992.htm) describe purchases of all target common shares with subsequent mergers. Both fall within the proposed window and disqualify these companies as negative controls. Positive announcement evidence does not establish the entire listing interval or pre-observation risk-set status.

## Required next evidence

For each proposed control, enumerate the complete issuer/regulatory announcement inventory across the full twelve months, including tender offers, mergers, terminated agreements, bankruptcy, listing changes and other censoring events. Review target-versus-buyer roles and distinguish whole-company control from licenses, equity financings and asset transactions. Record historical source availability separately from the 2026 review timestamp.

For unfinished companies, missing matches among the 49 reviewed positives remain **unknown**, never zero. A negative window also needs a verified historical security/listing interval through its horizon, or an explicit censoring treatment. Current survival and two isolated historical snapshots do not establish either condition. `validation_contract_ready_records` remains empty, with all training flags false.

Raw SHA-256 values refer only to bytes actually downloaded. The twenty per-record hashes identify these review receipts; they are not hashes of unavailable source HTML. No programmatic SEC requests were made.
