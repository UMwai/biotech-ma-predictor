TO EMPLOYEES – March 6

 

Hi Everyone,

 

We issued a press release this morning announcing our strategic combination with TCR2 to create a preeminent cell therapy company to treat solid tumors.

 

We will hold a joint webcast for investors with Dr. Garry Menzel, TCR2’s CEO, at 8 a.m. EST / 1 p.m. GMT. We encourage you to join and it can be accessed HERE (with a replay available at that same address).

 

This is a stock-for-stock transaction by which TCR2’s shareholders will hold 25% of the combined company’s stock and no cash payment will be exchanged. Importantly, upon completion of the transaction, this strategic combination extends our cash runway into 2026 which will enable delivery of key catalysts over the next two years.

 

We have complementary technology platforms, compelling clinical data, as well as a shared focus on solid tumors. Further, during the interactions with TCR2 we have come to know their leadership team well and believe that there are significant cultural synergies with a shared drive to make cell therapies a mainstream treatment option for people with cancer.

 

Going forward, I will be the CEO of the combined company, which will continue to be called Adaptimmune and trade under the same ticker – ADAP. Given the circumstances – I want to be clear upfront that we do not anticipate further restructuring within Adaptimmune because of this deal.

 

We will hold a company update after the webcast and answer any questions then. This is an exciting development for us, and I look forward to a lively discussion.

 

Best,
