Item 8.01 Other Events.

 

On March 17, 2022, Crinetics Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (the “Company” or “Crinetics”) announced that the Company now expects to release preliminary data from the ongoing Phase 1 study of CRN04894 in the second quarter of 2022 due to the fact that some of the healthy volunteers contracted COVID-19 during a recent cohort of the multiple ascending dose portion of the study.

 

The Company still expects to announce data from the multiple ascending dose cohorts of its Phase 1 healthy volunteer study of CRN04777 by the end of the first quarter of 2022.
